# LNG Freight Cost Sources & Drivers

## Internal Sources
- **Charter Rates**: Spot and time-charter rates from Platts and broker evaluations.
- **Fuel & Boil-Off Gas**: Based on vessel specs and voyage assumptions.
- **Canal & Port Fees**: Internal cost tables for Suez, Panama, and regional ports.
- **Insurance & Demurrage**: Shipping contracts and liability coverage.
- **Forecasting Systems**: Endur integration for P&L and risk management.

## External Benchmarks
- **Platts LNG Freight Assessments**: Daily hire rates for TFDE and two-stroke vessels.
- **Spark Commodities Indices**: Spark25S (Pacific) and Spark30S (Atlantic).
- **Baltic Exchange BLNG Indices**: For LNG FFAs and major trade routes.
- **Market Intelligence Reports**: Thunder Said Energy, Global LNG Hub.

## Cost Calculation Formula
**Total Freight Cost = (Charter Rate × Voyage Days) + Fuel Cost + Canal Fees + Port Charges + Insurance + Ballast Costs**

| Component           | Source/Benchmark                     |
|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Charter Rates       | Platts, Spark, Baltic Exchange      |
| Fuel & Boil-Off     | Internal voyage models, vessel specs|
| Canal & Port Fees   | Internal cost tables, Platts calc   |
| Insurance/Demurrage | Internal shipping contracts         |
| Forecasting         | Endur system, LNG Trading workflows |
